Steel Fabrication and Erection

Steel is decided in the connections. Design them early and the frame goes up in the order the crane wants.

We detail connections to the fabricator's shop standards rather than a generic library, which cuts weld volume and keeps pieces inside the transport envelope.

Erection sequence is designed alongside the frame, with the temporary stability case checked at every stage, so nothing on site waits on a stability check that was never done.
