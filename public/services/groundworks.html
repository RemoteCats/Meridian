Groundworks and Foundations

The cheapest foundation is the one that matches the ground investigation, not the one that matches the last job.

We specify and interpret ground investigation, then design the foundation that suits it, whether that is a raft on improved ground or driven piles to bedrock through liquefiable fill.

Settlement is checked against the structure above, because a slab that moves within tolerance still cracks a facade that does not.
