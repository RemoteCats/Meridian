Underwater and Marine Welding

A wharf does not come out of the water to be repaired. The repair goes down to it, and the weld has to pass the same inspection as one made in a shop.

Our dive teams work surface-supplied to AWS D3.6M class A and B, on pile jackets, wharf structures, intakes, gates and subsea pipe. Wet welds where the class allows, a dry habitat where it does not.

Every dive is planned with the structural engineer who designed the repair, so the diver is not interpreting a drawing at thirty feet. Inspection is video-logged and ultrasonically verified, and the record goes to the owner with the as-built.
