Oil and Gas Facilities

Nobody builds a refinery from scratch any more. The work is adding to one while it runs, and the shutdown window is the whole programme.

We design and build tankage, piping and structural steel inside operating facilities, to API, ASME and the operator's own standards, with every weld traceable to a procedure and a welder.

Turnaround work is planned backwards from the window that actually exists. Tie-ins are rehearsed on the model, spools are fabricated and tested before the unit comes down, and the critical path is measured in hours rather than days.
