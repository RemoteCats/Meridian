Commercial Construction

A commercial building is decided by the things nobody photographs: the frame, the shafts, and how early the trades can start.

We take commercial work from entitlement drawings through to the certificate of occupancy, holding the frame, envelope and systems as one package rather than three that meet on site and argue.

Most of our commercial work is on constrained urban sites, next to something that has to stay open. Logistics, crane positions and street closures are designed at the same time as the structure, because on a tight site the build sequence is the design.
