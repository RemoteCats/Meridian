Canyon Ridge Logistics Centre

A portal framed distribution centre taken from ground investigation to handover in sixteen months.

Perris, CA Completed 2024 Client: Logistics developer 16 months

A large shed is a simple structure with an unforgiving programme. Connections were detailed to the fabricator's shop standards rather than a generic library, steel arrived in erection sequence, and the slab was poured in bays that followed the frame rather than the other way round. The ground beneath it needed 40,000 cubic yards of engineered fill before any of that could start.
