Vista Bay Tank Terminal

Four API 650 tanks, their containment and the manifold that ties them in, built inside a terminal that never stopped shipping.

Wilmington, CA Completed 2024 Client: Terminal operator 19 months

Two tanks came down and four went up on the same footprint, with the terminal loading trucks through the whole programme. Ring wall foundations were designed for liquefiable fill with stone columns beneath; shells were built by the shell-jacking method to keep the welding at ground level. Every seam was vacuum-box and radiograph tested, and the manifold was hydrotested before the first tie-in was cut.
