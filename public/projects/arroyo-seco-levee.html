Arroyo Seco Levee Works

Three miles of levee raised and re-armoured, with the channel kept live through two storm seasons.

Los Angeles County, CA Completed 2024 Client: Regional flood control district 29 months

The levee was built to a rainfall record that no longer holds. We modelled the channel against the flow it will see in fifty years, raised and re-armoured three miles of embankment, and rebuilt eleven outfall structures along it. Everything in the channel had to be out or flood-ready by the first of November each year, which set the programme more than any other constraint.
