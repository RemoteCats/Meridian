Energy project

Sierra Vista Cogeneration Plant

A combined-cycle plant and its switchyard, tied into a transmission line that stayed energised throughout.

Kern County, CA On site Client: Independent power producer 27 months

The plant adds 48 MW and a heat recovery loop to a site that already exports power, so the connection could not be dropped for longer than a single planned window. Structures sit on drilled piers below the active zone of soils that swell through a forty degree annual range. Steel was galvanised and match-marked in the shop, and energisation was staged bay by bay against the live line.
