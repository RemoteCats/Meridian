A process upgrade with 38 tie-ins into live systems, executed inside an eleven-day window offshore.

Gulf of Mexico Completed 2025 Client: Offshore operator 18 months

The upgrade needed 38 cuts into systems that could only be opened during the annual shutdown, on a platform where every tonne of spare deck space had to be booked in advance. Spools were fabricated, tested and laid down in erection order onshore, each tie-in was rehearsed against the scan-registered model, and the sequence was written so no two cuts competed for the same crane.
